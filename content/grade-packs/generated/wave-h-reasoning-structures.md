# Grades 1–9 Wave H reasoning structures

- Grade 1: 1 public reasoning structures; One applied combine-groups step; Grade 1 has no retained source-backed multi-step slice in the immutable runtime.
- Grade 2: 10 public reasoning structures; Applied one- and two-operation measurement structures with explicit unit conversion or comparison.
- Grade 3: 12 public reasoning structures; Exactly specified two-step operation chains with independently checked intermediate results.
- Grade 4: 6 public reasoning structures; Two- or three-step exact rational chains with positive-domain and context constraints.
- Grade 5: 6 public reasoning structures; Two- to four-step integer chains with every dependency and unit public.
- Grade 6: 6 public reasoning structures; Multi-step rate/base/percentage chains with exact finite decimals and declared precision.
- Grade 7: 6 public reasoning structures; Distinct rational operation chains with complete units and nonzero denominators.
- Grade 8: 6 public reasoning structures; Forward, inverse and composed linear-function reasoning with nonzero coefficients and complete units.
- Grade 9: 6 public reasoning structures; Construct, solve and context-filter quadratic roots using fully public integer parameters.
